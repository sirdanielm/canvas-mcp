# Goals, methods and current work

The aim is dependable grading support that saves teacher time while preserving
original evidence, academic policy and teacher control. This page owns the SDM
agenda and capability summary; linked guides own procedures. The current scoped
work order determines assignments; d0t coordinates work in its queue. Detailed
case history, spending, installed pins and readback receipts stay private.

## Priorities

After repository maintenance, prioritize a functioning Unit 3 test grader
pipeline: qualify real intake, reuse saved stages and verify the receiving
result through the existing owners. Admit each source, form, policy, identity
and attempt through its current qualification path.

Continue proving the Unit 2 Sheet and local grading routes, advancing Unit 1
and CER under their own source, form and policy contracts, and continuing
formative intake and participation review. Work can proceed
independently when its required evidence and authority are available; the user's
current direction sets priorities. Reuse valid saved results and record genuine holds.

Make the retained private Canvas library the known discovery point for new
formative scans and electronic test submissions. Join curriculum metadata on
verified `course_id + assignment_id`; keep course assets, submission evidence,
gradebook exports and generated previews distinct. Preserve originals, capture
history and recorded hashes while reusing unchanged attachment versions. A
newer partial capture must not replace the last capture accepted as complete
within its recorded Canvas scope.

For each grading route, bind evidence to the source, physical attempt, form and
policy; preserve supported score and feedback, and verify the receiving result.
A prior canary is evidence for its own case, not proof of another route.

New model work is eligible only when Canvas is ungraded **and** no applicable
successful durable model result exists. Eligibility alone does not clear source,
policy, tool or spending limits. Preserve the existing **15-minute participation
window**; use the actual governing policy for its timing and calculation rather
than inferring those rules from this page. Retain Edpuzzle exclusions and teacher
decisions. Numeric `0` and `EX` are meaningful states, not missing evidence.
Keep **all published Canvas grades unchanged** unless the exact change is approved.

## Grading flow

Both intake lanes—Canvas and teacher-folder/paper evidence—retain original bytes
and provenance. Bind source, identity, assignment, form and policy to the actual
physical attempt; observe any Canvas attempt independently. A paper reassessment
label never supplies a Canvas attempt number.

| Work | Review and local staging | Canvas delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formative participation | Current evidence join → source-bound participation proposal and private analysis → QC → UNAPPROVED central EDIT draft and readback | Separate fresh comparison and exact approval; **score-only** under the existing policy |
| Summative results | Literal reading → policy-bound scoring and canonical itemized feedback → qualified accepted-decision or unapproved-proposal EDIT route and readback | Separate score and full-feedback permissions, durable intents and independent readbacks |

Staging an ordinary draft creates neither teacher acceptance nor publication
authority. Participation analysis is private, not a student comment. Score parity
does not prove feedback delivery. Retain readable feedback when the numeric total
is held; do not coerce it into an accepted numeric pair. A feedback-only EDIT
route requires its own actual qualification.

## Evidence and recovery

Source merge, installed binding, scientific QC, teacher acceptance, EDIT readback
and Canvas score/comment delivery are separate facts. Reuse valid authorization
and unchanged qualification within their actual scope; no generic per-stage
project confirmation is added here. Actual tool/security/data gates still apply.

Quarantine only affected cases or genuinely shared resources and continue
independent eligible work. Preserve teacher fields, higher-grade guards,
original baselines, failed reads and all completed/uncertain operation histories.
Reconcile a possible send through its original history. Do not replay or rekey
a completed or uncertain operation; use the applicable recovery path.
The newest failed or held evidence cannot be replaced by an older clean receipt.
